# RUN-009 Subtraction Report

## Status and scope

Complete. This is an exploratory synthesis audit of the 24 already selected sources and completed pilot reports. It adds no evidence and does not edit protected synthesis text.

## Subtraction rule

A construct survives only to the extent that removing it would make prediction, intervention design, rival discrimination, or typed comparison worse. Vocabulary-level coverage is not sufficient.

## Claim-level subtraction

- **C-001:** Survives only as an operationalized intervention claim. Remove any reading that observed instruction or recognition effects establish semantic necessity.
- **C-002:** Survives as the clearest programme-wide test. The required carrier-by-content and held-out comparison remains undone.
- **C-003:** Has no demonstrated incremental value. It remains bounded rather than supported.
- **C-004:** Has explicit rivals and falsifiers, but no positive attractor evidence. It remains rivalled.
- **C-005:** Has a concrete institutional recursion case, but the semantic mechanism is unresolved. It remains rivalled.
- **C-006:** A decomposed control model is a serious rival to unification. It remains rivalled.
- **C-007:** Relation typing prevented errors across the selected cases and remains preliminarily tested, not retained universally.
- **C-008:** A thin-protocol/domain-model hybrid is a serious rival to substantive compression. It remains rivalled.

## First-pass result

The architecture loses the least explanatory value when reduced to two methodological commitments:

1. semantic grouping must earn status through carrier-by-content invariance and incremental intervention or prediction beyond domain-specific rivals;
2. cross-scale similarities must be typed before evidence is transferred.

Effective fields, attractors, recursion, target-family unity, and compression remain research hypotheses with different maturity levels. None should be used as a summary of what the pilots established.

## Gate decision

No synthesis edit is validated for direct application in RUN-009. The two best clarifications touch protected commitments, while the remaining candidates either lack the required maturity advance or overgeneralize from one domain. Because no protected change is being proposed for application, no decision card is opened.

The safe output of RUN-009 is therefore the subtraction report itself: a documented interpretation rule for the final harvest. The synthesis remains unchanged, and the exact protected questions can be revisited only after carrier-by-content tests, fitted field comparisons, dynamical perturbation evidence, or independent held-out cases exist.

## Residual uncertainty

- The subtraction criteria privilege intervention and prediction over other possible explanatory virtues.
- Negative or missing evidence in a bounded corpus is not evidence that a construct can never earn support.
- The synthesis was not line-edited, so readers must consult this research record for the current empirical qualifications.
- Independent cases could alter the balance between a thin protocol and a substantive shared basis.

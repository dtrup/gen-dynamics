## Strongest findings

1. **Semantic effects are not semantic necessity.** Instructions can change fear, avoidance, expectancy, and some learning signals; recognized or intrinsically worthless monetary tokens can change exchange. These effects defeat pairing-only or intrinsic-value-only stories but do not defeat propositional, expectancy, coordination, infrastructure, incentive, or enforcement rivals.
2. **Carrier-by-content testing is the strongest shared empirical rule.** Meaning-equivalent content must generalize across carriers, meaning-different uses of similar carriers must diverge, and semantic grouping must improve held-out intervention or prediction after component controls.
3. **Recurrence is not an attractor result.** The selected studies show relapse and persistence, not autonomous return, switching thresholds, barriers, or hysteresis.
4. **Cross-scale typing has practical value.** Defensive circuits are not conscious fear; functional similarity is not mechanism identity; monetary recognition is not settlement infrastructure or political power.
5. **Thin protocol plus domain models currently beats substantive unification.** The common case protocol improves question discipline, but domain-specific variables retain the supported predictive and intervention detail.

## Preserved negative and unresolved results

- No pilot established carrier-invariant semantic causation.
- No effective-field model was fitted or shown to outperform component models.
- No semantic-attractor criterion was satisfied.
- No common formation-propensity estimator was available.
- No unified target family outperformed typed decompositions.
- No compact shared basis was complexity-matched or tested on held-out cases.
- Fiat-money interpretation was not isolated from trust, search, accounting, taxation, regulation, settlement, and asymmetric power.
- Laboratory fear and avoidance findings have limited clinical and long-horizon generalizability.

The protected synthesis therefore remains unchanged, no decision card is open, and this harvest—not a silent synthesis rewrite—records the current empirical boundary.

## Evidence and inference limitations

- Source selection was purposive and capped, not systematic; each pilot contains exactly eight sources.
- Full-text inspection was uneven, and several findings rely on abstracts or authoritative metadata.
- Authors, paradigms, and theoretical traditions overlap within pilots, limiting independence.
- Laboratory timescales dominate the threat and conditioning pilots; the monetary pilot is more theoretical and institutional, so perturbation quality is not comparable.
- No new data were collected, no model was estimated, and no effect sizes were synthesized.
- The common protocol originated in the synthesis under evaluation and may favor its vocabulary.
- Claim maturity reflects bounded testing in this repository only and should not be read as publication-grade confirmation.

## Final programme finding

The programme did not validate a substantive generative-dynamical architecture across the selected cases. It produced a narrower gain: a discriminating test for semantic causal claims, a useful cross-scale typing discipline, explicit rivals for field/attractor/unification/compression claims, and cleanly stated experiments that would be required to advance them.
